    Projection: R. Buckminster Fuller's icosahedral (Airocean) world map, Gray–Fuller formulas via d3-geo-polygon. Land and railways: Natural Earth. Shipping lanes: traced with searoute over Eurostat's maritime network between major container ports (indicative, not live). Flights: 2,500 busiest direct routes from the OpenFlights database (2014, ODbL). 3D: three.js. Stars and constellation figures: d3-celestial (BSD). Planets from JPL's approximate orbital elements; Moon from the Astronomical Almanac series (both within ~0.2°). Sun position computed from the Astronomical Almanac formulas (≈0.01°).
